# MindBridge
### A Digital Mental Wellness Platform for University Students

MindBridge is a student-first digital mental wellness platform designed to support university students in managing stress, emotional well-being, and the challenges of transitioning into college life.

## 🌉 About MindBridge

MindBridge aims to provide students with a safe, accessible, and supportive digital space where they can understand and manage their emotional well-being.

The platform brings together self-care tools, wellness resources, peer support, mentor connections, and access to professional help in one place.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Promote mental wellness among university students.
- Help students identify and understand their emotional state.
- Provide accessible self-care and relaxation resources.
- Connect students with seniors, mentors, and counselors.
- Create awareness about mental health and available support systems.
- Reduce the hesitation associated with seeking help.

## 📖 How to Use

### 1. Access the Platform

Open the MindBridge application using the deployed link or run the project locally.

### 2. Create an Account

New users can create an account using the Signup option.

### 3. Login

Existing users can log in to access the MindBridge dashboard.

### 4. Explore the Dashboard

After logging in, users can access the available mental wellness services from the dashboard.

### 5. Check Your Mood

Use the Mood Check-in feature to reflect on your current emotional state.

### 6. Practice Guided Breathing

Use the Guided Breathing feature to practice simple breathing exercises and relaxation techniques.

### 7. Use the AI Wellness Companion

Students can interact with the AI Wellness Companion for digital wellness support and guidance.

### 8. Connect with Support

Students can explore:

- Senior Buddy Connect
- Mentor Connect
- Counselor Directory

These features help students find appropriate sources of support.

## 🎓 Target Users

MindBridge is primarily designed for:

- University students
- First-year students
- Students experiencing academic or transition-related stress
- Students looking for accessible mental wellness resources
- Students seeking peer, mentor, or professional support

## 🌍 Social Internship Context

MindBridge was developed as a social-impact project focused on understanding and addressing mental wellness challenges faced by university students.

As part of the Social Internship, field engagement activities were used to understand community needs and gather relevant feedback.

The findings from the field engagement are documented separately in the Field Work Report and supported by documentary evidence.
